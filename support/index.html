Help with Vites and how to reach us.

Vites doesn't record my drives

Open iOS Settings → Vites and set Location to Always, and turn on Motion & Fitness and Background App Refresh. In the app, Profile → Drive detection shows what is missing.

Does Vites drain my battery?

Vites only uses precise GPS while you drive and rests otherwise. Continued GPS use in the background can still reduce battery life, especially on long drives.

Manage or cancel Vites Pro

In the app go to Profile → Vites Pro, or in iOS Settings tap your name → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. Bought on another device? Use Restore purchases on the Vites Pro screen.

Hide my home or work

Profile → Privacy → Privacy zones. The start and end of drives inside a zone are never shown to friends.

Delete my account

Profile → Delete account removes your account and all server data. Drives that exist only on your phone stay there until you delete the app.

Contact us

Didn't find your answer? Open a request and we'll reply there. Please don't include personal data such as your email address or location, as requests are public.
