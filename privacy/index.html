Privacy Policy

What Vites collects, why, and your choices.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Vites records your car drives automatically and turns them into a map, statistics and shareable cards. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Location and motion. To detect and record drives, Vites uses your device's location (including in the background) and motion activity. Drives are stored on your device first. Without an account, they never leave your device.
  • Account data (only if you sign in). Email address, username, display name and an optional profile photo. If you add a phone number, we store only a one-way hash of it so friends who have your number can find you.
  • Drives you upload (only if you sign in). Route points, start and end times, distance, speeds, place names of the start and end, and the road cells you passed (used for your heatmap). Tags and notes you add to a drive are visible only to you.
  • Vehicles and fuel prices you enter, used to estimate fuel costs.
  • Contacts (optional). If you allow it, phone numbers in your contacts are compared against hashed numbers to suggest friends. Your contact list is not stored.
  • Live location (optional). If you turn on live sharing, your current position while driving is visible to your friends.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive the subscription status, never your payment details.

How we use it

Only to provide the app's features: recording drives, your map and statistics, social features with friends you accept, cost tracking and the Vites Pro subscription. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Who can see your data

  • Friends you accept, only for what you share with them (heatmap, trips, live location). You choose this in Profile → Privacy.
  • Privacy zones you set hide the start and end of drives near those places from everyone else.

Service providers

  • Supabase (EU, Frankfurt): database, sign-in and file storage.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status.
  • Nominatim / Photon (OpenStreetMap): turning start and end coordinates into place names.
  • OpenFreeMap: map tiles.
  • Apple: payments and notifications.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete a single drive from its detail screen, or your whole account in Profile → Delete account, which removes your server data and profile photo. Drives stored only on your device are removed when you delete the app.

Children

Vites is not intended for children under 16.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: see the support page.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
